## What is SIF 
"SIF is a small fraction of light emitted when excited chlorophyll molecules return to their ground state after absorbing sunlight. SIF is mechanistically linked to photosynthesis and stress responses and can detect physiological changes earlier than conventional optical indicators, often before visible symptoms emerge."
